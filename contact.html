Common questions

Is the trial lesson really free?+

Yes. Your first online lesson is free, so you can see if I'm the right tutor before you commit.

Are lessons online or in person?+

Everything is online: 1-on-1 lessons, Masterclass sessions and the free trial. You can join from anywhere.

How much do lessons cost?+

1-on-1 lessons are $50 an hour for Years 9–10, $60 for Years 11–12, and $70 for Extension 2. 90-minute lessons are $65, $80 and $90. Masterclass members get 1-on-1 lessons for $40 an hour.

Can I get help between lessons?+

Yes. If you're stuck on a question, message me and I'll help outside lesson time too.

What's the Masterclass?+

A term program for Year 12 Advanced, Extension 1 and Extension 2, built on my method, for $60 a week. It starts in Week 5, depending on demand. You get small group sessions twice a week, a weekly workbook that I mark, and individualised help. Spots are limited.
